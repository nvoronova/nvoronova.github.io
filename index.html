Nadya, she/her

Hi! I am a postdoc at IRIF in Paris, working with Frédéric Magniez. Before that I did my PhD at Boston University with Mark Bun.

I study what computers, and specifically quantum computers, can and cannot do when a resource is scarce: when memory is small (quantum streaming), when the input can only be accessed a little at a time (quantum query complexity), or when it is split between machines that can exchange few qubits.
